To Thangoo,

written the way it came to my head

scroll slowly ↓

I don't really know how to write this.
I don't know how to make it sound good
or make it rhyme
or make it look like one of those poems
people actually know how to write.
So I'll just write it
the way it came to my head.

You came into my life
and somehow became a part of it
without even asking.
No big entrance.
No warning.
No “hey, I'm going to become important to you.”
You just... came.

And then one day
I realized I was checking my phone
hoping your name would be there.

And then you went offline.
Not for a day.
Not for two.
For a whole month.

And I swear
that month felt weird.
It felt like being stranded somewhere
with nothing in my hands
and nowhere to go.
Not literally.
But you know that feeling
when you keep walking
and you don't even know where you're supposed to reach?
Yeah.
That.

Every time my phone made a sound
I looked at it.
Every notification.
Every single one.
And for that stupid little second
my brain would go,
“Maybe it's her.”And when it wasn't,
I'd just put my phone down
and continue whatever I was doing.
Like nothing happened.

But I still checked again.
And again.
And again.
I don't even know why I did that so much.
Actually...
I do.
It was because I missed you.

Then my birthday came.
And somehow
you made that day feel different.
You gave me something
I still don't know how to explain properly.
That letter.
I read it
and I laughed.
Then I cried.
Then I stared at it.
Then I read it again.

And somewhere in between all of that
I realized something really stupid.
I didn't just miss talking to you.
I missed you.

And maybe that's why
your little messages mean so much to me.
“Good morning thangoo.”“Good night thangoo.”“Lobe you alotttuuu.”Those might just be words to someone else.
But to me...
they became little pieces of proof
that somewhere in this giant world
there was someone
who remembered me.
Someone who checked on me.
Someone who cared whether
I slept properly or not.
Someone who would still be there
when I disappeared into my own head.

And then you told me
“I won't cage you like a bird...
I just want you to remember
that I'm here always.”I don't think you understand
what that sentence did to me.

Because I think
that's all I've ever wanted from people.
Not someone to hold me.
Not someone to keep me.
Just someone
who says,
“Go wherever you want.
I'll still be here.”

And you somehow became that person.
You became the person
I want to tell random things to.
The person I look for
when something happens.
The person whose notification
can change my entire mood.
The person I can talk to
at the most random hour
about the most useless thing
and somehow it still feels important.

And yeah...
sometimes I get scared.
Scared that one day
life will get busy.
Scared that people will change.
Scared that we'll stop talking
as much as we do now.
Scared that I'll wake up one day
and your name won't be
somewhere in my notifications anymore.

Because I've already had people
walk out of my life.
And I don't think
I want to watch you become
another person I used to know.

You're not just another person to me, Thangoo.
You're the person who showed up
when I didn't even realize
how badly I needed someone.
The person who made me feel remembered.
The person who somehow made
a really ordinary life
feel a little less ordinary.

And maybe someday
we'll look back at all this
and laugh at how dramatic I was.
Maybe we'll have completely different lives.
Maybe we'll be busy.
Maybe we'll talk less.
Maybe everything will change.

But I hope...
somewhere in all that change,
there's still a little part of us
that remembers this.
The stupid conversations.
The random messages.
The “thangoo”s.
The late nights.
The days we didn't talk.
The days we talked too much.
The month I kept checking my phone
hoping it was you.
The birthday
where you made me feel special.
And this stupid poem
I wrote
because apparently
I don't know how to say things normally.

You once told me
to remember that you're there.
So I will.
And if someday
you forget how much you mean to me,
I'll remind you.
Not because I owe you anything.
Not because you have to stay.
But because you're you.
And you're my Thangoo.

And I don't know what the future looks like.
I don't know where life takes us.
I don't know how many people
will come and go.
But for as long as I'm allowed
to have you in my life,
I'll be here.

I love you. And I won't leave you. ❤️
